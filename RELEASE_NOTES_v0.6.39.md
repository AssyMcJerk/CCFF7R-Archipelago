# CCFF7R Archipelago – Alpha v0.6.39

First public alpha. Beatable start to finish; tested over three full runs.

**Attachments**
- `ccff7r.apworld` – put it in `Archipelago/custom_worlds/` (requires Archipelago 0.6.x)
- `Crisis Core Reunion.yaml` – options template
- `ccff7r_poptracker_0.3.12.zip` – PopTracker pack (maps + autotracking)

**Checks:** story chests, chapter clears, every mission reward and mission chest, minigames (each can be
excluded), and optionally shopsanity, DMW characters/memories, the Magic Pot, Buster Sword proficiency, mailsanity
and NPC mission handouts.

**Goal:** collect the 7 Goddess Materia and defeat Genesis.

**Known limitations**
- Chapter gating sends you back to the field when you reach a chapter's ending without its key. Chapters 6–8 use
  special handling (Ch6 resumes the ending once the key arrives); report anything odd with your client log.
- Saves are tied to their seed; use a new save per seed (`/claimsave` overrides).
- Steam build only.

Thanks to DrMisunderstood for testing.
